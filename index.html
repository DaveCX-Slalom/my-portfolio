05 Design process

A non-linear process,
accelerated by AI.

Feedback and decisions move faster with AI, and are validated from day one. Taste and judgment still belong to the designer.

01

Discovery & alignment

Stakeholder workshops, problem framing, and business goal alignment. I help teams use AI to externalize ideas before sessions.

02

Platform & user research

Audit the platform stack and design system alongside user research, defining what’s possible and what’s customizable up front.

03

Concept & AI prototyping

Move directly from concept to a working prototype. The prototype is the wireframe—and it’s testable from day one.

04

Validation

Review with stakeholders and test with users on something they can actually click. Let evidence guide decisions.

05

Design system application

Apply tokens, components, accessibility, and polish after direction is validated. The prototype becomes production-ready design.

06

Handoff & design QA

In modern stacks, the prototype’s front end can start the engineering work. Handoff becomes closer to a code review than a spec dump.

Phases 3–5 cycle two or three times before production, with workshops revisited at major decision points. AI accelerates prototyping and handoff; it never replaces the judgment needed for discovery, research, and validation.
